Before you buy

IC coin packs are bought through Apple or Google, which handle payment. We do not receive your payment details.

  • Coins are stored on your device.
  • If you delete the app or move to a new device, your unused coins are lost and cannot be restored.
  • Please spend or keep your coins on the device where you bought them.

How IC coins work

  • Coins are only used to start game runs.
  • They have no cash value.
  • They cannot be redeemed for money.
  • They cannot be transferred to another person or account.
  • They do not expire.

If a pack you paid for was not added to the app, email us. Tell us whether you bought it from Apple or Google, and the approximate date and time.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google through their own refund process. We do not issue refunds directly.

  • Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: Request a refund on Google Play

Reset or delete data on your device

Your settings, mute choice, scores, avatar look, coin balance and age band are stored only on your device. We do not keep a copy.

  • RESET AVATAR clears your avatar look only. Find it on the AVATARS screen (open it from the title screen) and in the avatar builder.
  • There is no reset-all control in the app.
  • Deleting the app removes all CRT Buddy data from your device, including any unused coins.
