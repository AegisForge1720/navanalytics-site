What this policy covers

This policy describes how information is handled in the CRT Buddy app and on this privacy page.

CRT Buddy is a retro arcade game app for a general audience. It is not directed to children under 13. The store listing is aimed at people 13 and older.

When CRT Buddy is offered in the App Store or Google Play, it is planned as a paid app you buy once, with optional IC coin packs bought in the app. Apple or Google would handle that payment.

Information stored on your device

You do not need an account to use CRT Buddy.

These items are stored only on your device: your settings, mute choice, game scores, avatar look, coin balance, and an age band. The age band is never a birth date. Nothing in this list is sent to us.

The avatar builder lets you start with a penny character, or build a look with sliders and preset pieces. The app does not use the camera or your photos, and it has no AI avatar.

Removing your data

On the AVATARS screen (open it from the title screen), and in the avatar builder, RESET AVATAR clears your avatar look only. There is no reset-all control in the app.

Deleting the app removes all CRT Buddy data from your device.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information through the CRT Buddy app. The app does not send us your data.

The app has no third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising software.

This policy covers the CRT Buddy game played in a web browser. Before CRT Buddy is offered in the App Store or Google Play, we will update this policy to cover those versions.

If and when IC coin packs are offered through the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google will handle payment. We will not receive your payment details. Apple and Google process purchases under their own terms and privacy policies.

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data.

This website

The service that hosts this website may automatically log technical information such as IP address and browser type for security and reliability, under its own retention policies.

If you email us

If you email us, we use your email address and the content of your message only to reply. We keep that information no longer than needed, and for no more than 12 months.

Children

CRT Buddy is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.

New York law

The laws of the State of New York, United States, govern this policy.
