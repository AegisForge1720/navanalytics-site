Our Origin Story

A completely made-up history, told with a completely straight face.

Landlord Larry was built on a cartoon hill on a sunny morning, with one chimney, two windows and a very good mustache. He was proud of his rooms and polite to everyone. Then, one evening, he noticed a line of ants walking along his kitchen floor. They had not asked. They had not signed anything. Larry was shocked.

He did not shout. He did not spray anything. He found a clipboard, put on his bowler hat, and walked over to the ants. "Excuse me," he said. "I believe the rent is due." The ants, after a long discussion, offered him a crumb. Larry took the crumb, wrote a receipt and thanked them very much. They did not leave.

From that day on Larry knew his calling. He founded No Free Rent Pest Control, the world's first and only pest control company that works entirely by asking nicely. He recruited a team: Clause the Clipboard, Ledger Lou, Deposit Doris and Notice Ned. None of them have ever removed anything.

Today Larry still knocks on skirting boards every evening, still accepts crumbs as a goodwill gesture and still says please. The company remains imaginary. The domain, however, is real, and Larry has prepared a very short, very polite lease for its next owner.
