Hello down there! Welcome to Get Out Of The Ditch, the only ditch company that would really rather you stayed a while. Our host is Wendell Wiggleworth, a friendly pink earthworm in a yellow hard hat and a green tour-guide vest, who carries a little wooden ladder and a small orange flag everywhere he goes.

Wendell does not pull anyone out of anything. Wendell has never towed a car, rescued a truck or done anything involving engines. Wendell gives tours of his ditch, which he considers the finest ditch in the land. This is a parody website, no services are offered and nothing is for sale except the domain name. If you or your vehicle are really stuck, please call a real, qualified roadside or towing service.

Please wiggle along to the joke menu, meet the crew and read reviews from fans who do not exist. And if you ever really end up in a ditch, Wendell says to call a real roadside service first. He will save you a spot on the tour for later.
