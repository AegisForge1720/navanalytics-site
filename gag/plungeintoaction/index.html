LISTEN UP, CHAMPIONS. Welcome to Plunge Into Action, home of Coach Chuck Suction: a wooden handle, a red rubber cup, a sweatband, and more enthusiasm than any household object has a right to. Chuck is not a plumber. Chuck is not a coach. Chuck is a cartoon. But nobody has told Chuck that, and frankly we are scared to try.

Chuck believes that everybody has a little blockage between where they are and where they want to be. His solution is always the same: get a good seal, push down with purpose, pull up with pride, and repeat until something happens or lunch arrives, whichever comes first. It has never been tested on anything, because this is a parody website and nothing here is for sale except the domain name.

Browse the menu, meet the squad and read the reviews from fans who do not exist. Then stand up, stretch, and give yourself a round of applause. Chuck would want that. Chuck would want it LOUDER.
