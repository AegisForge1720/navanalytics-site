Attention, please! Single file! Welcome to Shingle File Roofing, home of the most orderly roofing shingles in the entire cartoon world. Our leader is Drum Major Shelby Shingle, a brick-red shingle in a tall plumed hat who believes that every roof is really just a very well-organised queue.

Shelby's method is simple: line up, wait your turn, overlap the shingle in front of you politely, and never, ever push. It is a beautiful system. It has also never been used on a real roof, because this is a parody website and nothing here is for sale except the domain name.

Please proceed in an orderly fashion to the joke menu, meet the marching crew, and read reviews from fans who do not exist. Remember to hold the door for the person behind you. Shelby is watching, with her whistle.

Why Choose Us (Imaginarily)

Impeccable Queueing

Our shingles have never cut in line. Not once. Shelby has the clipboard to prove it.

Marching Band Precision

Every shingle steps in time. It sounds lovely. It achieves nothing on a roof.

Zero Actual Roofs

We have marched past many roofs. We have climbed none of them. Ladders make Shelby nervous.
