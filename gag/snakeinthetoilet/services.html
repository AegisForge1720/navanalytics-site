We Knock Before We Pop Up.

Our 13 signature pretend services are listed below, roughly in order of how proud we are of them. None of them can be ordered. All of them can be imagined, which is honestly the better deal.

All Pretend Services

The Polite Pop-Up

Sir Reginald rises from a cartoon bowl, tips his tiny blue cap, apologises for the intrusion and sinks back down with dignity.

Price: One sincere "excuse me"

The Hiss-terical Flush

A cartoon snake waves hello with the tip of his tail while a cartoon handle makes a whooshing noise. That is the whole service.

Price: One heartfelt apology, any topic

The Lid Courtesy Knock

Three gentle knocks from beneath the lid, followed by a pause long enough to be considered polite in four countries.

Price: Knock back once

The Bow Tie Inspection

Sir Reginald straightens his bow tie in the reflection of the water and asks whether it looks even. It always does.

Price: A compliment on the bow tie

The Monocle Survey

Each tile is inspected through a monocle and pronounced "rather fine". No tile has ever failed.

Price: One "jolly good"

Afternoon Tea at the Tank

A tea set balanced on the tank lid. Two sugars. Pinky extended. He has no pinky, but the spirit is there.

Price: Two sugar cubes, imaginary

The Coil-Up Combo

Two bow ties, one pun, zero actual work, delivered in a perfect spiral.

Price: A high five (left hand)

The S-Bend Serenade

A gentle tune hummed from somewhere in the S-bend. Acoustics are excellent. Lyrics are mostly the letter S.

Price: Polite applause, two claps maximum

The Seat Up, Seat Down Summit

Sir Reginald chairs a calm, minuted household discussion about the seat. He abstains from voting.

Price: One family vote, by show of hands

The Boa Builder

Builds an elaborate imaginary pipe sculpture, then admires it, then politely forgets where he put it.

Price: Your best dad joke

The Sssspecial

Whatever you imagine, but sillier, and with more S sounds than strictly necessary.

Price: Three compliments to a houseplant

The Thank-You Note

After every imaginary visit, a handwritten note arrives in the drawing: "Thank you for having me. Lovely grout."

Price: A stamp drawn in pencil

The Full Noodle Package

Every service above, in order, with a bow between each one. Runs roughly as long as a short opera.

Price: A standing ovation, performed seated
