Good day, and welcome to Snake In The Toilet, the most well-mannered imaginary plumbing firm on the entire internet. We are not a real company. We do not have a van or a single pipe. We do have one cartoon wrench, which Sir Reginald holds for photographs. What we have is Sir Reginald Coilsworth: a long green cartoon noodle with a red bow tie, a tiny blue cap, and the manners of a retired butler.

Sir Reginald believes that every bathroom deserves a little dignity. So in our drawings he knocks three times on the lid, waits a respectful moment, rises slowly while humming something classical, tips his cap, compliments the tiles, and then excuses himself. That is the entire business model. It has never made a penny, because nothing here is for sale except the domain name itself.

Below you will find our menu of noodle-based services, each priced in compliments, apologies and good posture. Please browse at your leisure, and do mind the bow tie.
