Privacy Policy

Operator: Insert Coin Industries LLC (formerly NAV Analytics LLC). Effective date: November 23, 2026.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Insert Coin Industries LLC, a New York limited liability company. Insert Coin Industries is the parent company name for our brands. The company was formerly NAV Analytics LLC.

For privacy questions or requests, email andrew@insertcoinindustries.com. This is the same contact address used for other inquiries. Our mailing address is 58 Owaissa Dr, Rochester, NY 14622.

2. What this policy covers

This policy describes how we handle personal information in connection with this company website. The site is an information page about Insert Coin Industries and its brands. It does not offer accounts, checkout, or paid subscriptions at this time. Before any app, subscription, or payment collection begins, this policy will be updated to describe that collection and sharing, and the revised policy will be posted before those features are offered.

3. What we collect

We do not use analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking technologies of our own on this website, and we do not set cookies of our own. There is no cookie banner because this site does not set cookies of its own. There are no account forms and no contact forms on this site.

Videos. Some pages let you play short videos hosted on YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com, run by Google). Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play. When you do, YouTube loads the video in a player on our page and may set cookies or similar storage and receive your IP address, device information and viewing activity, under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive that information. If you do not want that, do not play the videos. Our social media links (X, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram) leave this site; those services apply their own policies once you click.

If you email us, we receive the email address and the content of your message, along with any information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to you and to keep a record of the conversation.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). The hosting provider may automatically log technical data such as IP address, date and time, and the requested URL, for security and operations, under that provider's own terms.

We do not currently process payments or operate user accounts through this website. We will not store full payment-card numbers. If and when paid products are offered, payment information will be handled by a payment processor such as Stripe or PayPal, and this policy will be updated to name the processors actually used before that collection begins.

4. How we use information

  • To respond to email and other requests you send us, including privacy requests.
  • To operate and secure the website.
  • To keep business and legal records.
  • To comply with law.

We do not use this website for advertising measurement, and we do not sell personal information.

5. How we share information

We may share information with service providers who help us operate email, who are permitted to use the information only to provide that service. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which receives technical data when you visit the site and handles it under its own terms and privacy statement. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger, sale, or reorganization of the business, subject to appropriate protections.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with sportsbooks or with sellers of lottery tickets.

6. Retention

Email correspondence is kept until the request is resolved and for a limited archive period afterward, which we expect to be up to three years unless a longer period is required by law or needed for an ongoing matter. Technical data logged by our website host, GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), is kept under GitHub's own terms and retention practices, which we do not control. We dispose of private information when it is no longer needed, in a way that is reasonable for a small business under the New York SHIELD Act.

7. Your choices and rights

You may email andrew@insertcoinindustries.com to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or to withdraw a request. You do not need an account to make a request. We will not discriminate against you for making a request. We aim to respond within 45 days. If a right you ask about does not legally apply to us, we will say so and will still honor deletion and similar requests as a courtesy where we reasonably can.

We may not currently meet the thresholds of the California Consumer Privacy Act or similar state laws. We still describe our practices accurately and offer the requests above as a courtesy. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to a small business under the New York SHIELD Act. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe there has been an unauthorized use of information we hold, please email us.

9. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Draw Buddy and Reveal Buddy are for adults 18 years of age or older. This company website is a general information site for the business and is not directed at children.

10. People outside the United States

We are a United States company, and information is processed in the United States. This site is not directed at the European Union or the United Kingdom.

11. Automated decisions

We do not make legal or similarly significant decisions about you using automated processing on this website.

12. Changes

When this policy is first published, we will add an effective date. If we make material changes, we will update the policy and the "last updated" date. If we begin to collect new categories of information, or start payments or accounts, we will post an updated policy before that collection begins.
